A 9-phase AI pipeline that preserves document context

Rather than sending your document as a raw text dump, WalnutAI section-aware chunks it, vectorizes it, and uses RAG to pull the most relevant sections as context for each extraction call. The result is stories that reflect the document's actual structure and intent — not a flat summary.
